Destruction and deletion

There are two independent steps for destroying this workspace and the infrastructure associated with it. First, its Terraform infrastructure should be destroyed. Second, the workspace in Form2Stack, including its runs, variables, settings and state history, can be deleted.

Manually destroy

Queuing a destroy plan will redirect to a new plan that will destroy all of the infrastructure managed by Terraform. It is equivalent to running terraform plan -destroy -out=destroy.tfplan followed by terraform apply destroy.tfplan locally; the plan always asks for confirmation before it is applied.
